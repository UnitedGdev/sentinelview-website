Data & Account Deletion

SentinelView is designed to give users a clear way to request deletion of information associated with their use of the service.

How to request deletion

Before launch, this page will provide the active support contact or account-deletion workflow used to process requests.

Development status

The final deletion workflow and contact address will be published before public release and will match the actual SentinelView implementation.

What deletion may cover

  • SentinelView account information, where applicable.
  • Authorization information held by SentinelView.
  • Application data retained by SentinelView, subject to legal requirements and legitimate security records.

Questions

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This development-stage policy is intended to describe SentinelView's planned data practices. Before public release, it must be reviewed and updated so it accurately reflects the final implementation and services used by the app.

1. About SentinelView

SentinelView is an independent application developed by United Gamers. It is designed to provide an organized interface for authorized access to compatible Ring device information and supported media.

2. Information we may process

  • Account and authorization information needed to connect a Ring account.
  • Device metadata, device status, capabilities, and configuration information made available through authorized Ring APIs.
  • Supported images, video clips, live video information, and event information when requested by the user.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide requested features, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, provide support, and operate the application. We do not intend to use security-camera content for unrelated advertising.

4. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate SentinelView, subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

We aim to retain information only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose or as required by law. Users may request deletion through our data and account deletion page.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

7. Children's privacy

SentinelView is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children without appropriate adult supervision.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

Privacy contact details will be added before final publication.
